Group project · Cybersecurity Lead

DWK Personal Finance Management System

A group project designing a fictional personal finance management system aimed at everyday users. I worked as the Cybersecurity Lead on the team.

My focus was the security side of the system: thinking through trust and privacy from a user's perspective, working out sensible permission levels for different types of data, and identifying where the design could introduce risk. I then explained these security considerations in plain language so the rest of the team and other stakeholders could follow the reasoning behind our decisions.

University coursework · Research

Computing & Society Digital Artefact

A university project exploring how AI tools can affect communication and collaboration skills among first-year university students. I drew on existing research and my own experience to think through the benefits and risks of AI in education — including where it can genuinely support learning, and where it might reduce student interaction instead.

The project involved structuring an argument clearly, backing it up with research, and writing in an academic style.
